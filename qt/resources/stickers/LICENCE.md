# Licence of the built-in stickers

The sticker collections in this folder (circuit symbols, logic gates, 3D solids, lab glassware; every `.xopp` file,
`names.json` and `collections.json`) and their generator `generate.py` are the xournal-qt project's own work. They
are dedicated to the public domain under **CC0 1.0 Universal**
(<https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/>): copy, change and use them for anything, without asking and
without naming us.

The drawings follow the usual textbook conventions (IEC 60617 style circuit symbols, the distinctive-shape and the
IEC rectangular logic gates, oblique projection of solids); they are drawn from scratch by `generate.py`, not copied
from a standard or a book.
