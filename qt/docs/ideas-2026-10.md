# Ideas: what other apps have, and what only this app can do (2026-10-04)

A comparison with GoodNotes 6, Notability, Drawboard PDF, Xodo, PDF Expert, OneNote, Samsung Notes, Nebo,
Obsidian (with Excalidraw and Canvas), Zotero's reader, reMarkable, Apple Notes and Preview, and Okular, made on
2026-10-04 against what the app had then (ROADMAP, TODO). Items the author chose to build are marked **(chosen)**
and have blocks in [TODO.md](../../TODO.md); the others are kept here to decide later. Sizes: S about 1–2 days, M
about 3–5 days, L more.

## A) Small things users of those apps expect

| # | Idea | Seen in | Builds on | Size |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A1 | **Hold to straighten** **(chosen)**: finish a stroke and pause, it becomes a straight line, circle or rectangle | GoodNotes, Notability, Apple Notes, Samsung Notes | upstream's `ShapeRecognizer` (today a separate mode of the Shapes button), a pause timer in `CanvasInput` | S |
| A2 | **Scratch out to erase** **(chosen)**: a zigzag over ink deletes it (opt-in) | GoodNotes, Samsung Notes, Nebo, Apple Scribble, OneNote | a zigzag test when the stroke ends; the erase/delete undo actions | S–M |
| A3 | **Zoom window**: write large in a magnified strip, a frame on the page shows where, it moves on along the line | GoodNotes, Notability, Samsung Notes | the self-reference view with its edit toggle; a frame like the curtain's | M |
| A4 | **Copy handwriting as text, or turn it into a Markdown box** | GoodNotes, Nebo, Samsung Notes, OneNote | the readings `qt/hwr-search` stores (`ink-text.pack`, `qt/src/hwr`) | S (copy), M (convert) |
| A5 | **Dark reading mode**: pages inverted on the GPU, pictures kept | Xodo, PDF Expert, Okular, Drawboard, Zotero | a shader where `DocumentCanvasItem` composes the tiles (no page drawn again) | S |
| A6 | **Insert pages from a PDF file** | Preview, PDF Expert, Xodo, GoodNotes, Drawboard | the `qt/pdf-pages` merge, pasting pages between tabs | S |
| A7 | **Extract or split selected pages** into a new document or PDF | Preview, PDF Expert, Xodo | the page selection, the qpdf merge, the CLI's `--export-range` | S |
| A8 | **Export pages as PNG** | GoodNotes, Notability, OneNote, Samsung Notes | upstream's image export, `qt/src/render/RegionRender` | S |
| A9 | **Sticky notes and Markdown boxes as comments in other PDF apps** (`/Contents`, `/T`, `/M` on the stamps the hybrid PDF writes) | Acrobat, Xodo, Drawboard, Zotero | the hybrid PDF's `/Stamp` annotations | S |
| A10 | **Find and replace** in `.md` files and Markdown boxes **(chosen)** | Obsidian, every text editor | the search bar, the editor's undo steps | S |
| A11 | **Quick note** **(chosen)**: one tap, a shortcut or `--quick-note` makes `Inbox/<date time>` in the library, or adds to today's `.md` | Apple Quick Note, OneNote, Samsung Notes, Obsidian daily notes | "New document", `SingleInstance`, an Android launcher shortcut | S |
| A12 | **Templates from your own pages** **(chosen)**: "Save page as template", chosen when adding a page; optionally with the page's PDF background, so it is the same as copying a PDF page | GoodNotes, Notability, Samsung Notes, reMarkable | the `Stickers/` folder model (a `Templates/` folder beside it), page backgrounds, page copy/paste | M |
| A13 | **Tags** **(chosen)**: `#tag` in typed text and Markdown, XMP keywords in PDFs; indexed per folder, a chip like Favourites, `tag:` in the fuzzy syntax | Apple Notes, Samsung Notes, Obsidian, Zotero | the per-folder index, the fuzzy query | M |
| A14 | **Presenter view on a second screen** **(chosen)**: slide with its note space, the next slide, a timer | Okular, PowerPoint | the presentation mode, two views of one document, `qt/note-space` | M |
| A15 | **Read aloud**, the word being read marked like a search hit | Okular, Edge, Xodo, Samsung | Qt TextToSpeech over the text index (on Linux it needs speech-dispatcher: against "self-contained", skip where missing) | M |

## B) What only this app can do (in the spirit of Markdown inside a PDF with notes)

| # | Idea | Why it fits this app | Size | Risk |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| B1 | **A PDF that explains itself**: every PDF with notes carries a `notes.md` attachment, rewritten on save: typed text, the best handwriting readings, annotations and links per page with `#page=` anchors | any indexer, LLM or tool that reads PDF attachments gets the notes without the app; the `.md` attachments (`qt/md-pdf`), the annotations export and the handwriting readings exist | M | privacy and size: an option |
| B2 | **The CLI as an agent tool, then MCP over stdio**: `search <library> "query"` (fuzzy syntax, handwriting included), `cat doc.pdf --page 3 --md`, `render --page 3 --dpi 100`, `backlinks`, `append note.md` | the per-folder index is plain files, no server; VISION's "Further out" | M (+2 days MCP) | the index code lives in `xqt-shell`; writes while the app runs go through `SingleInstance` |
| B3 | **Live query blocks in Markdown**: ```` ```xqt-query tag:exam todo:open``` ```` shows matching documents and pages with page pictures, to-dos and backlinks | Dataview over PDFs and handwriting; other Markdown apps show a code block | M | low |
| B4 | **Page portals**: a live region of another document's page embedded in notes (`![[paper.pdf#page=4&rect=…]]`); other viewers get a cached picture plus a link | `qt/links`, `RegionRender`, the snip link offer | M–L | upstream compatibility, stale pictures |
| B5 | **Handwritten wikilinks**: write `[[Name]]` or circle a word; the handwriting reading, matched fuzzily against library names, suggests a link | a handwritten Zettelkasten on `qt/hwr-search` and `qt/links` | M | accuracy: suggest, never link automatically |
| B6 | **Version history inside the PDF**: the revisions incremental saves append become "Versions in this file" (preview, restore, compare a page), with a setting for how many to keep | the history travels with the file, no sidecar | S–M | privacy (Share already compacts), conflicts with the 25 % compaction |
| B7 | **A folder as one shareable notebook PDF**: one hybrid PDF, each document a chapter in the outline, links between them as links inside the PDF, originals embedded; "Unpack to folder" | extends the archive export | M | the links' round trip |
| B8 | **Adopt annotations from other apps**: highlights, ink and text that Acrobat, Zotero, Xodo or Preview added become editable (with consent) and show in the Annotations panel | the hybrid PDF stays the truth after others marked it up | M–L | fidelity, duplicates on the next save |
