  • coefficients fall off as 1/k

Convergence and the Gibbs phenomenon

  • For finite energy over one period the partial sums converge in the mean (Parseval).
  • At a jump they overshoot by about 9 % of the jump, however many terms are taken.
  • Between jumps they converge pointwise to x(t).
