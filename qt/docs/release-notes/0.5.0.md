Fifth alpha of **xournal-qt**, a Qt 6 fork of Xournal++ for pen tablets and convertibles. This one is about **your
own tools**: a toolbox of the pens you pick up, stickers and templates, to-dos and tags across the library, audio
recordings, handwriting you can search, and a canvas that turns like paper on a desk. It is a pre-release: much of it
has only been tested off-screen so far (see "Known limits").

## Your tools

- **My tools**: the tool bar is now a toolbox of your own pens, highlighters, shapes, erasers, laser pointer, text box
  and sticky notes, each with its colour, width, line style and fill, in your order with dividers. It sits on the right
  by default and can be docked to any side; in full screen and while presenting it floats, on a phone it is the dock.
  Tap a tool to take it, tap it again to change it, hold it for its menu or to move it; "+" adds one. The old tool
  bar stays in Settings → Pen → Tools as "Classic".
- **Colour palettes**: Classic, Marker, Pastel study, Colorblind-safe (8 and 6) and Dark, as tabs in the colour
  chooser, with named roles (body, warnings, key terms, examples, definitions, headings, questions, ideas). Tools that
  took a palette colour follow when you switch palettes.
- **Line styles and fill**: dashed, dash-dot and dotted lines; shapes and closed strokes filled with their colour or
  another one.
- **Laser pointer**: ink that fades after half a second (a setting), never saved.
- **Hold to straighten**: pause at the end of a stroke and it becomes a line, rectangle, circle, ellipse or triangle.
  **Scratch out** (off by default): a quick zigzag over ink erases it.
- **The pointer** is a real cursor now, a dot (or the crosshair, a setting), as fast as the system's own; the eraser
  shows its real size.
- **Undo and redo** lead the tool bar, and the redo button works while writing Markdown.

## Teaching and presenting

- **Curtain (B) and spotlight (Shift+B)**: hide part of the page, or everything but a rectangle; move, turn and size
  them like the set square. Only on screen.
- **Presenter view**: with a second screen, the audience sees only the slide (with your ink, laser and curtain live),
  and your screen shows the slide with its notes, the next slide, a clock and a timer.
- **Reading**: ⋮ → View → Read shows the page alone and read-only, with sideways or vertical scrolling, snapping to
  pages or free.
- **Turn the canvas**: two fingers or the touchpad turn it like paper (it snaps upright and to quarter turns),
  Ctrl+] / Ctrl+[ turn it by 90°, a double tap or the fit buttons turn it back.

## Collecting and reusing

- **Stickers**: save a selection (optionally with the PDF behind it) to the library's `Stickers/` folder or the
  app-wide set, and paste it back from the sticker picker. **Groups**: Ctrl+G and Ctrl+Shift+G group and ungroup;
  pasted stickers are groups.
- **Page templates**: save a page as a template (with its PDF page if you want) and add it from the add-page button,
  Insert pages or New document.
- **Snip**: Shift+S / Shift+L copies the picture of a rectangle or lasso; pasted into another document it offers a
  link back to its page.
- **Quick note**: Ctrl+Alt+N, the home screen or `xournal-qt --quick-note` makes a note in the library's `Inbox/`,
  or a line in today's Markdown note.
- **Pages**: hold a page in the sidebar or the grid to select it; turn pages by 90° (PDF pages too, also in `.xopp`
  files, where Xournal++ shows them turned as well).

## Finding things

- **To-dos**: Markdown task lines marked `todo:` (or every check box, a setting) from all documents of the library, in
  one list grouped by document or folder, with due dates (`📅 2026-10-12`). Tick them there; add them to your
  calendar; a check-box stamp for handwritten to-dos.
- **Tags**: `#tags` in your text and notes, and the keywords of PDFs, in a Tags tab and as `tag:name` in search;
  "Tags…" writes a PDF's keywords.
- **Handwriting search** (Linux, English, off until you switch it on in Settings → Search): your handwriting becomes
  searchable, fuzzily, and other PDF apps find the words too in PDFs with notes. The model (64 MB) is downloaded once
  when you switch it on.
- **Find and replace** (Ctrl+H) in Markdown and text documents, with case, whole word and regular expressions.

## Audio

- **Recordings** tied to what you write, as in Xournal++ (whose files keep playing there): tap a stroke with the play
  tool to hear what was said while it was written. A recording is also a voice memo of its page. In a PDF with notes
  the recordings are attachments named by their pages (`audio-p012-p015-….ogg`), so they can be found without the app.

## Markdown and the rest

- **Tables** are laid out like in a browser: no more columns squeezed to single letters.
- **Sharper at 125 %, 150 % and 175 %**: thumbnails, the selection, lines and frames on whole pixels.
- **Getting started**: a short introduction on the first start, and a tutorial document to write on (Help).

## Known limits

- **Built and tested off-screen in one go**: twenty blocks of this release were developed in a cloud session and
  tested with the off-screen test suite; the device round (pen feel, gestures, the presenter view on a real projector,
  recording on real microphones, Android and Windows) is still ahead. See `qt/docs/testing/device-checklist.md`.
- Handwriting search is English only, Linux only, and needs the model download; recording needs Qt Multimedia
  (packages that lack it offer no recording).
- The Android launcher shortcut for quick notes, the calendar hand-off and the recording service were not built for
  Android in this round.
