Tenth alpha of **xournal-qt**, a Qt 6 fork of Xournal++ for pen tablets and convertibles. A short one after 0.9.0:
the built-in handwriting search now reads **handwriting at an angle as well as level handwriting**, the **library's
search finds handwriting**, **pasted text is readable** again, and **pinching no longer turns the canvas** by accident.
It is a pre-release: see "Known limits".

## Handwriting search

- **Handwriting at an angle is read as well as level handwriting**: a line turned by 90° (a note up or down the
  margin) is now found whole and read exactly as the same ink level; before, real handwriting at 90° fell apart into
  several pieces, most of them read as level text (about a fifth of the letters wrong). Also new: text written
  **upside down** (leftwards, 160°–180°) and leftwards at a slant (110°–160°) is read upright, a long line written
  **uphill at 10°–20°** keeps its own angle instead of being cut into pieces, and a **margin note of two words** is
  enough. Measured on the handwriting benchmark with the built-in model: at ±90° and 180° the same reading as level;
  at 15°–45° and ±135° one line per line with about 11–13 % of the letters read differently (before: 2–18 pieces per
  line, up to 85 % different). Handwriting at an angle that was read before is read again once.
- **Fixed: the library's search found no handwriting** that Ctrl+F found in an open document. A document's
  handwriting reached the library only when it was saved after every page had been read. Now a document of the
  library goes there once its handwriting is read, saved or not, and saving hands over what is read so far. The
  library's documents that are not open are read in the background on mains power again on a desktop with a wireless
  mouse, keyboard or pen, whose battery had counted as the computer's.

## Pen and touch

- **Pinching no longer turns the canvas by accident**: two fingers turn it only after a deliberate twist of about
  65° (a large pinch twists the fingers a little on its own). Once it turns, it follows the fingers, also back to
  smaller angles, and still snaps to 0°, 90°, 180° and 270°. The touchpad's rotate gesture is unchanged.
- **Fixed: pasted text could be invisible**: text pasted onto a page (handwriting copied as text, or text from
  another app) takes the text tool's color. It took the color of the tool in hand, so with the lasso of "Copy as
  text" it could be white, and with the highlighter yellow.

## For contributors

Handwriting forms to copy out by hand (`qt/research/hwr/forms/pdf/`: English, German, and both) turn a person's
handwriting into training data and into a benchmark of the whole handwriting search, angles included:
`xournal-qt-cli hwr-form` and `xournal-qt-cli hwr-bench` (`qt/research/hwr/forms/README.md`). Sharing filled forms is
not set up yet.

## Getting it

| Platform | File |
| --- | --- |
| Linux (KDE neon, Ubuntu 22.04) | `xournal-qt_0.10.0_amd64_neon-jammy.deb` |
| Linux (Debian 13, Ubuntu 25.04 and newer) | `xournal-qt_0.10.0_amd64_debian13.deb` |
| Any Linux | `xournal-qt-0.10.0-x86_64.AppImage` |
| Windows 10/11 (x64) | `xournal-qt-0.10.0-windows-x64.zip`: unpack anywhere and start `xournal-qt\bin\xournal-qt.exe` (no installer yet; SmartScreen may ask first: "More info" → "Run anyway") |
| macOS 15 or newer, Apple Silicon | `xournal-qt-0.10.0-macos-arm64.dmg`: not signed yet — on first start macOS refuses; then System Settings → Privacy & Security → "Open Anyway" (`qt/docs/development/macos.md`) |
| Android 9 or newer (64-bit ARM) | `xournal-qt-0.10.0-android-arm64.apk`: open it on the phone; it installs over 0.9.0 and keeps the library and the settings |

As in 0.9.0, every package carries ONNX Runtime and the handwriting model (for non-commercial use only: the
`LICENCE.md` in its folder), so the handwriting search needs no download.

## Known limits

- **Handwriting at an angle** was tuned and measured on one writer's handwriting; other hands at a free angle may be
  read less well. A column of single letters or digits can pass for a line written downwards; large headlines are
  still left out as drawings.
- **Tested off-screen only**: Android's back button and gesture and Zen on the phone and the Fold; the twist
  threshold for turning the canvas (65°) has been tried with simulated fingers, not yet on a tablet.
- **Android: the top bar scrolls into a blank area at its end** (from 0.8.0, not fixed yet).
- The plain autosave still runs on the UI thread; a very large document may pause the pen for a moment when it is
  autosaved.
- From before: comparing versions shows which pages changed, not yet what changed on them; old versions are never
  thinned out; replay shows ink appearing, not erasing or moving it.
