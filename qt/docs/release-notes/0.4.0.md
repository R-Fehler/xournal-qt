Fourth alpha of **xournal-qt**, a Qt 6 fork of Xournal++ for pen tablets and convertibles. This one is about
**every screen size**: the whole interface adapts from a wide desktop to an upright Surface and a folded phone, and
the first **Windows** and **Android** builds are part of the release.

## One interface for desktop, tablet and phone

- **Size classes**: the window knows whether it is a wide or narrow desktop, an upright tablet (a 2-in-1 or a Surface
  Pro at 200 %, the unfolded Galaxy Fold), a phone upright or sideways, or tiny, and lays itself out for it. Choices
  you make (the sidebar shown, where the tool bar goes) are remembered per size; Settings → Display → "Adapt the
  layout to the window size" turns it all off.
- **Tool bar**: it fills the space it has. With room, every colour and width is shown; with less, the widths become
  one button, the colours your recent ones plus a palette, and rarely used buttons go into "more tools" (»). ⋮ never
  scrolls away. On an upright tablet the tools take **two rows** (at the top, or at the bottom by choice), so an A4
  page keeps its width.
- **Cycling buttons**: pen ↔ highlighter, the eraser's kinds, rectangle ↔ lasso, all shapes, set square ↔ compass
  share a button each: a tap on the active one moves to the next, a long press lists them all.
- **Clear icons without hover**: holding a finger on a button shows its name (and does not press it).
- **One place for each action**: the ⋮ menu is down to ten entries (Document, Export, Page, View as submenus) and
  repeats no tool bar button.
- **The view pill** is slimmer: the zoom percentage alone (a tap: fit width, 100 %, fit height, fit page; a double
  tap or a long press: fit page), the contents next to the page grid. The sidebar opens from an arrow at the page's
  edge, as a drawer over the page where there is no room beside it.
- **Menus and dialogs** fit every window; on phones menus and short questions come up from the bottom, forms fill
  the screen, and Settings is a list of sections.
- **Upright**: the Markdown source goes below the page, a reference above the notes.

## On a phone

- A slim **top bar** instead of the tab strip: the library, the document's name (swipe along it to switch documents),
  and the number of open documents (a tap: all of them; a double tap: the one before; a long press: the recently
  used ones).
- A **tool dock** at the bottom within reach of the thumb: the tool in use, all tools, colour, width, undo, redo and
  the page number.
- Controls keep clear of the gesture bar and camera cut-outs; while typing, the formatting bar sits right above the
  keyboard and the cursor stays in view. In very small windows the page is shown alone (a tap in the corner brings
  the controls back).
- **Typing with the on-screen keyboard** works on the page and in text boxes (it did not reach the page before).

## Writing

- **Enter** in Markdown now starts a new line of the same paragraph, **Enter twice** a new paragraph, Shift+Enter a
  paragraph at once — the same on every device, as in note apps.
- **Bookmarks and favourites**: star documents (the ★ in the library's header shows only them) and bookmark pages;
  the library's Bookmarks view shows the bookmarked pages of all documents. Bookmarks live in the document: in PDFs
  as outline entries every PDF viewer shows, in Markdown as `<!-- xqt:bookmark -->` comments.
- **Sticky notes**: a note's text is cut at the note while you type; several notes can be selected together with
  other ink (Ctrl+click, a rectangle, or "Select more" in the pill on a touch screen) and copied, moved or deleted
  together. Notes hold one Markdown text each and take pasted pictures.
- **Pages**: A0 to A7 paper, the size of existing pages can be changed, small lined pages keep their ruling in
  proportion; Markdown boxes can be resized; documents can be renamed from their tab and the library.

## Getting it

| Platform | File |
| --- | --- |
| Linux (KDE neon, Ubuntu 22.04) | `xournal-qt_0.4.0_amd64_neon-jammy.deb` |
| Linux (Debian 13, Ubuntu 25.04 and newer) | `xournal-qt_0.4.0_amd64_debian13.deb` |
| Any Linux | `xournal-qt-0.4.0-x86_64.AppImage` |
| Windows 10/11 (x64) | `xournal-qt-0.4.0-windows-x64.zip`: unpack anywhere and start `xournal-qt\bin\xournal-qt.exe` (no installer yet; SmartScreen may ask first: "More info" → "Run anyway") |
| Android 9 or newer (64-bit ARM) | `xournal-qt-0.4.0-android-arm64.apk`: open it on the phone (allow installs from the browser or file manager); later versions install over it and keep the library |

## Known limits

- The new layouts were tested on a Galaxy Fold 7 (folded and unfolded) and on desktop Linux; the Surface's upright
  layout and many phone details still need a round on the devices (`qt/docs/testing/device-checklist.md`).
- Windows has no installer and no file associations yet; macOS is not built yet.
- Handwriting is not searchable yet; audio recording, the plugin console and the LaTeX tool of Xournal++ are not
  ported.
