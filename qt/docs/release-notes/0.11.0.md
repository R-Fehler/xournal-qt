Eleventh alpha of **xournal-qt**, a Qt 6 fork of Xournal++ for pen tablets and convertibles. This one brings
**plugins in JavaScript** and, built on them, a **function plotter** for teachers and students: type a function, see
it on the page, insert it as ink with formulas as labels. Also new: **built-in stickers** for circuits, logic gates,
3D solids and lab glassware, **the recordings in the replay**, and recordings kept **next to their notes**. It is a
pre-release: see "Known limits".

## Plot a function

- **Plot a function…** (⋮ → Tools, the toolbox's "+" under Insert, Ctrl+Alt+P) opens a dialog beside the page and
  previews the plot on the page at once; drag its frame to place it, its corner to size it; **Insert** puts it there as
  one undo step. Type functions as on paper (`x^2 - 2x + 1`, `2x`, `sin x`, `sqrt x`, `1,5x`, `|x|`, `2pi`); a mistake
  is named under the field ("unknown function "sni" - did you mean sin?"). Several functions, **curves x(t), y(t)**,
  **parameters on sliders** (`a*x^2 + b`), colors from the palette, line width, dashed lines.
- Textbook axes: grid, arrows, numbers in steps of 1, 2, 5 or in **multiples of π**, axis names, the y range fitted
  automatically or typed; the formulas next to their curves or in a legend, written as math ($f(x) = x^2 - 2x + 1$);
  zeros, extrema and intersections marked. **Exact scale** for worksheets: 1 unit = 1 cm (or any mm), true to size
  when printed at 100 %.
- Poles and jumps (1/x, tan x) break the curve: no vertical lines.
- The plot is ink and Markdown boxes, one group: move, scale, color or erase parts like anything you wrote. Select it
  and choose **Edit plot…** to change it again. A plot you changed by hand (parts erased or moved) asks first, since
  editing draws it anew. In Xournal++ it is plain strokes and texts.
- **Copy and paste a plot** (in the same document or another one, as often as you like): each copy is a plot of its
  own that **Edit plot…** changes without touching the others.

## Plugins

- **JavaScript plugins** add commands to the menus, the toolbox and keys, with dialogs and a live preview on the page.
  They run in a sandbox (no files, network or programs) and may do only what you allow them the first time they need
  it; **Settings → Plugins** lists them, switches them on and off, and changes what each may do. Every command is
  **one undo step** (a note with Undo follows it), and a command that fails or runs longer than 2 seconds is undone.
- Plugins go into the app's data folder (`~/.local/share/xournal-qt/plugins` on Linux). The plugin guide, the API and
  how to port a Xournal++ Lua plugin: `qt/docs/features/plugins.md`. Xournal++'s Lua plugins do not run in
  xournal-qt.
- Four examples come with the app, off until switched on: Color cycle (Alt+C), Toggle grid, Layer actions, Export.
- `xournal-qt-cli plugin run` runs a plugin's command on documents without the app (for example worksheets with plots),
  with permissions given on the command line.

## Handwriting search

- **A better handwriting model**: the built-in model learned for longer and from more handwriting. On the test sets
  it finds 98.0 % of German words (was 97.5 %) and 91.3 % of English ones (was 90.3 %), at the same size and speed.
  The handwriting of a library is read again once with it.

## Built-in stickers

- The sticker picker has a third tab, **Built in**: collections that come with the app, for drawings that are slow
  to draw well by hand. **Circuit symbols** in IEC style (resistor, capacitor, diode, LED, transistors, sources,
  meters, switch, op-amp, ground, fuse and more), **logic gates** (the usual shapes and the IEC boxes), **3D solids**
  with dashed hidden edges (cube, cuboid, cylinder, cone, sphere, pyramid, prism, tetrahedron) and **lab glassware**
  (beaker, flasks, test tube, burette, pipette, measuring cylinder, Bunsen burner, condenser, funnel, benzene).
- A tap pastes one as a group, ready to move and scale; its leads meet the wires you draw. The search finds them by
  their English and German names ("Widerstand" finds the resistor).
- **In the pen's colour**: built-in stickers can take the pen's colour instead of black.
- **Hide a collection** you never use (press and hold its chip, or Settings → Documents → Built-in stickers); "Show"
  in the picker or "Restore hidden collections" brings it back.
- Built-in stickers are read-only: **Copy to my stickers** makes one of your own to change.

## Recordings

- **Hear the recordings in the replay**: ⋮ → View → Replay the writing plays a document's recordings with its ink, each
  at the moment the ink was written; a speaker on the play bar (or the key **A**) switches them off and on, and the
  choice is kept. Seeking, skipping and the next session follow; dragging the slider is silent until it is let go.
- **Recordings live next to their notes**: a `.xopp` keeps its recordings in a `name.audio` folder beside it, so moving
  or syncing the folder keeps the audio. A new document records into the app's folder until it is first saved, which
  moves them there; Save as copies them. Renaming, moving, copying, sharing and deleting a document in the library
  take the folder along, and the library does not show it as a folder. Recordings made with 0.10.0 or earlier stay in
  the app's audio folder and are no longer found by their documents.

## Known limits

- Plugins cannot be installed on Android yet (their folder is private to the app there); the function plotter comes
  with the app everywhere.
- Polar curves, empty coordinate systems, value tables, shading, tangents and number lines are planned.
- **Tested off-screen only**: the plotter's dialog and preview on a phone or tablet, the built-in stickers' tabs on a
  narrow phone, the replay with real sound, and plugins on Android, Windows and macOS. See the device checklist
  (`qt/docs/testing/device-checklist.md`).
- **Android: the top bar scrolls into a blank area at its end** (from 0.8.0, not fixed yet).
- The plain autosave still runs on the UI thread; a very large document may pause the pen for a moment when it is
  autosaved.
