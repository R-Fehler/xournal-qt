Draft notes for the next pre-release of **xournal-qt** (not released yet; the version is set when it is cut).

## Changes

- **Hear the recordings in the replay**: ⋮ → View → Replay the writing plays a document's recordings with its ink, each
  at the moment the ink was written; a speaker on the play bar (or the key **A**) switches them off and on, and the
  choice is kept. Seeking, skipping and the next session follow; dragging the slider is silent until it is let go.
- **Recordings live next to their notes**: a `.xopp` keeps its recordings in a `name.audio` folder beside it, so moving
  or syncing the folder keeps the audio. A new document records into the app's folder until it is first saved, which
  moves them there; Save as copies them. Renaming, moving, copying, sharing and deleting a document in the library
  take the folder along, and the library does not show it as a folder. Recordings made with 0.10.0 or earlier stay in
  the app's audio folder and are no longer found by their documents.
