## Built-in stickers

- The sticker picker has a third tab, **Built in**: collections that come with the app, for drawings that are slow
  to draw well by hand. **Circuit symbols** in IEC style (resistor, capacitor, diode, LED, transistors, sources,
  meters, switch, op-amp, ground, fuse and more), **logic gates** (the usual shapes and the IEC boxes), **3D solids**
  with dashed hidden edges (cube, cuboid, cylinder, cone, sphere, pyramid, prism, tetrahedron) and **lab glassware**
  (beaker, flasks, test tube, burette, pipette, measuring cylinder, Bunsen burner, condenser, funnel, benzene).
- A tap pastes one as a group, ready to move and scale; its leads meet the wires you draw. The search finds them by
  their English and German names ("Widerstand" finds the resistor).
- **In the pen's colour**: built-in stickers can take the pen's colour instead of black.
- **Hide a collection** you never use (press and hold its chip, or Settings → Documents → Built-in stickers); "Show"
  in the picker or "Restore hidden collections" brings it back.
- Built-in stickers are read-only: **Copy to my stickers** makes one of your own to change.
