## Replay how a document was written

- **The timeline**: ⋮ → View → Replay (or the replay button of the playback pill) shows a play bar at the bottom and
  replays the document as it was written, stroke by stroke, read-only: play and pause, scrub, speed, jump between
  sessions (a long pause starts a new one; shorter pauses are shortened). Recordings play along where they overlap
  the writing, so a lecture's ink appears as it was said. Tap a stroke to jump there. Leaving it leaves the document
  exactly as it was.
- Every new stroke, text, image and link now carries the time it was made (about 5 bytes per stroke in the file; a
  `xqt-created` attribute that Xournal++ ignores). Older documents and files saved by Xournal++ have no times: their
  ink comes first, in the order it lies on the page.
- Not yet: erasing, moving and page changes are not replayed (strokes appear where they are now); a stroke is drawn
  on at an even pace, not at the speed it was written.
