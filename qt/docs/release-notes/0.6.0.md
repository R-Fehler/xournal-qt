Sixth alpha of **xournal-qt**, a Qt 6 fork of Xournal++ for pen tablets and convertibles. This one is about
**time**: replay how a page was written, keep versions inside the PDF and compare them side by side, and protect
PDFs with a password. Plus a reworked reading mode and tool bar. It is a pre-release: much of it has only been tested
off-screen so far (see "Known limits").

## Replay how a document was written

- **The timeline**: ⋮ → View → Replay (or the replay button of the playback pill) shows a play bar at the bottom and
  replays the document as it was written, stroke by stroke, read-only: play and pause, scrub, speed, jump between
  sessions (a long pause starts a new one; shorter pauses are shortened). Recordings play along where they overlap
  the writing, so a lecture's ink appears as it was said. Tap a stroke to jump there. Leaving it leaves the document
  exactly as it was.
- Every new stroke, text, image and link now carries the time it was made (about 5 bytes per stroke in the file; a
  `xqt-created` attribute that Xournal++ ignores). Older documents and files saved by Xournal++ have no times: their
  ink comes first, in the order it lies on the page.
- Not yet: erasing, moving and page changes are not replayed (strokes appear where they are now); a stroke is drawn
  on at an even pace, not at the speed it was written.

## Versions inside the PDF

- **Version history** for PDFs with notes (off by default; switch it on in the sidebar's History tab, ⋮ → Document →
  Version history…, or for every new PDF with notes in Settings → Documents). The file keeps one version per day you
  worked on it, plus **milestones**: Ctrl+Alt+S (Save with a message…) keeps a version with your message for good.
  The PDF as you received it is the first version.
- **Look back**: the History panel lists the versions (milestones only, if you like) and how much room they take; a
  version opens read-only beside the document, can be restored (as a new version on top, one undo step) or opened as
  a copy.
- **Still one ordinary PDF**: the versions are the PDF's own incremental updates. Every PDF app shows the latest one,
  and the latest notes are a plain `document.xopp` attachment any PDF tool can extract; older versions are stored as
  small deltas (`xournal-qt-cli export-xopp file.pdf --version N` gets any of them back).
- **Sharing** sends a copy without the versions unless you tick "With its version history". Documents that keep
  versions show a clock on their library card.
- Not yet: comparing two versions and playing through them; thinning old versions out (all are kept).

## Side by side

- **Locked scrolling** in the reference view (the lock in its pill): two documents, a document beside itself or a
  version beside now scroll, turn pages and zoom together, paired by page with the offset they had when locked;
  pages of other sizes line up. Remembered per pair.
- **Compare versions**: in the History panel, "Compare with now" or "Compare with another version…" shows the two
  side by side, locked; the pages that changed are marked (also added and removed pages), with previous / next
  change. Versions are read-only wherever they are shown.

## Password-protected PDFs

- **Open** PDFs that need a password (it is kept in memory for the open document only); PDFs with only
  restrictions open as before and their print and copy restrictions are honoured.
- **Protect** a PDF with a password (⋮ → Document → Protect with a password…, AES-256), optionally with printing,
  copying or editing restricted; change or remove it later; Share can send a protected copy.
- Saves stay fast and version history works on protected PDFs. Nothing readable is left in the app's cache: the
  autosave is encrypted too, protected PDFs show a lock in the library and are not indexed.
- Xournal++ cannot open protected PDFs: "For Xournal++" asks you to remove the password first. An archive (PDF/A)
  export cannot have a password; its dialog says so.

## The tools

- **Read only** replaces the reading mode: in full screen or while presenting, ⋯ → Read only (Ctrl+Alt+R; ⋮ → View →
  Read goes straight there) hides the tools, writes nothing, and a tap on the left or right fifth of the page turns
  to the previous or next page. A lock in the corner gives the tools back; Esc leaves.
- **Snip** is a tool you can put in the toolbox ("+" → Snip): a tap takes a rectangle or lasso screenshot, a tap
  while it waits switches the shape.
- **The command bar** at the top offers Read, Replay, Milestone (where the document keeps versions) and Tags when
  there is room.
- Fixed: dashed and dotted line styles showed as plain lines in the tool's editor and on the toolbox; choosing
  something in a tool's editor moved it to the top left of the window.

## Known limits

- **Tested off-screen**: these features were built and tested with the off-screen test suite; the device round
  (replay smoothness, read only's tap fields with a pen, Acrobat and Preview opening protected PDFs and PDFs with
  versions, printing protected documents) is still ahead. See `qt/docs/testing/device-checklist.md`.
- Version history: comparing shows which pages changed, not yet what changed on them; old versions are never thinned
  out; files with older encryption (RC4, AES-128) keep no versions.
- Replay shows ink appearing, not erasing or moving it.
- Handwriting search reads English only until the German model is published.
