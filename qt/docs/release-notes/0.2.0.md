Second alpha of **xournal-qt**, a Qt 6 fork of Xournal++ for pen tablets and convertibles. Its files are still
Xournal++ files; new here is that a document can also be a single PDF that every PDF app shows with its notes.

## PDFs with notes

- **Save as "PDF with notes"**: an ordinary PDF that any viewer shows with the ink (as annotations with their exact
  look), which also carries the full Xournal data, so it opens here again with every stroke editable.
- **Ctrl+S appends only what changed** (a standard PDF incremental update): on a 1,300-page PDF a save takes about
  0.15 s instead of seconds. Share and export always write a fresh file, so no deleted ink travels along.
- **PDF files or Xournal++ files**: the first start asks how you keep documents. In PDF mode new documents are PDFs,
  notes on a PDF go into that PDF (the way Drawboard PDF and GoodNotes work) and nothing is written next to your
  files. Changeable in Settings → Documents.
- **Export for the archive**: PDF/A-3b (checked with veraPDF), with the ink flattened into the pages and the Xournal
  data embedded; also for a whole library.
- Share: the PDF with notes, a copy for Xournal++ (`.xopp` + PDF), or onto the clipboard.
- Pages pasted from another PDF stay PDF pages (searchable text), and saving no longer blocks the window.

## Libraries and search

- Any folder can be a library; recent libraries; a "Show" filter; Markdown files, images and other files in the
  library (other files open in their own app).
- A small cache folder per library folder (or all of it in the app cache), so big libraries open at once.
- Fuzzy search (fzf-like, with logical operators) behind a toggle, word by word in the text, with typo tolerance
  (Settings → Search) and a help page (long press on the toggle).
- A text index per open document: search in long PDFs is instant and keeps what you typed.
- Links between documents (to a page, a chapter or a heading), with backlinks, kept when documents are renamed.
- Open documents follow changes other programs (or sync apps) make to their files; sync conflict copies show as a
  "Conflict" badge with compare and keep-one.
- Names sort naturally ("notes 2" before "notes 10") and without regard to case.

## Writing and reading

- **Markdown and text files** as documents: edit `.md` and `.txt`, as pages or continuous, "New Markdown file".
- **Reference view**: a second document beside your notes, to read or to write in, also as its own tab.
- Presenting, 16:9 pages, sideways scrolling, page number jump, fit width of the current page.
- **Draw with the finger** (a tool bar toggle), for devices without a pen.
- The setsquare and compass are drawn on the GPU: smooth at any size, also on high-DPI screens.
- PDF pages are rendered once for the screen's scale (four times fewer pixels on 2× screens); no grey pages after
  zooming; the pages in view first.

## Fixes

- A save that does not fit on the disk now fails and leaves the file as it was (Qt 6.7 reported success and cut the
  file).
- No crash at exit from the text reader; no crash on an empty path in the recent list.
- The window opens maximized and leaves full screen to the state it had before.

## Getting it

Linux: two `.deb` packages (KDE neon / Ubuntu 22.04, and Debian 13 / Ubuntu 25.04 and newer) and an AppImage; which
one fits is in `qt/docs/releasing.md`. qpdf 12 is now built into the app, so the packages no longer depend on the
system's qpdf.

Android (arm64) and Windows builds exist and are being tested on devices; they are not part of this release yet.

## Known limits

- Images and math in Markdown, Obsidian-style vaults: not yet.
- Audio recording, the plugin console and the LaTeX tool of Xournal++ are not ported.
