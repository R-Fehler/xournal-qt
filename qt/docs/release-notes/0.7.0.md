## Annotations from other apps

- A PDF marked up in another app (Preview, Drawboard PDF, GoodNotes' editable export, Acrobat, …) offers once to
  **make its annotations editable**: ink becomes strokes, highlights, underlines and strike-throughs become
  highlighter strokes, text boxes become text boxes, rectangles, ovals, lines, arrows and polygons become shapes,
  notes and comments become sticky notes, stamps and signatures become pictures. They go into a layer "From <app>" on
  each page; one undo brings the originals back. Also ⋮ → Document → Adopt annotations from other apps…
- Ink that other apps flattened into the page (GoodNotes' flattened export, Notability) stays part of the page.
  Pressure is not in the PDF standard, so adopted strokes have an even width.

## Pages in and out

- **Insert pages from a file** (the add-page button, ⋮ → Page, the page menu): from a PDF, a PDF with notes or a
  `.xopp`; all pages, a range or the ones you tick, before or after a page. PDF text stays searchable, ink comes
  along; one undo step.
- **Extract** selected pages into a new document (opened in a tab, optionally removed from this one) or **split** a
  document every N pages, at the selected pages or at its chapters.
- **Pages as pictures**: export pages as PNG or JPEG (72–600 dpi, transparent background if you like), and **Copy
  page as image** (Ctrl+Shift+C) puts the page on the clipboard at a high resolution (300 dpi by default, Settings →
  Storage → Pages as pictures), sharper than a screenshot. Exporting one page copies it too.

## Dark pages and paper colors

- **Dark pages** (⋮ → View → Dark pages: off, on, or with the system's dark mode): pages are shown dark without being
  drawn again; ink and highlighters are shown in their dark equivalents (palette colors become the Dark palette's),
  so notes stay readable in both; pictures keep their colors. Files, exports and printing do not change.
- **Paper colors**: white, illustration paper, kraft, soft green, soft blue, grey, dark grey and black, each also as
  **textured paper**, for new documents, new pages and a page's background. Ruled and graph lines stay visible on
  dark paper; the highlighter shows on dark paper too; a new document on dark paper takes light ink. Printing dark
  paper warns that it uses a lot of ink. Xournal++ shows the colors (without the texture).
