Draft notes for the next pre-release of **xournal-qt** (not released yet; the version is set when it is cut).

## One way to arrange the tools

0.8.0 reworks how the tools and commands are laid out: one arrangement, yours, the same on every screen from a wide
desktop to the folded phone.

- **Two bars, one arrangement**: the **rail** beside the page (your pens, highlighters, erasers, shapes, text boxes,
  notes, each with its color and width, and the hand, select, snip and mark PDF text) and the **top bar** (open, save,
  share, print, image, stickers, add a page, write on the page, the setsquare, the finger draws, record, search, read,
  replay, present, full screen, **Zen**, tags, favourite, bookmark, settings). Both keep your order on every screen.
- **Both scroll instead of folding**: when they do not all fit, a bar shows half of the next button and a fade where
  there is more; swipe it, or use the mouse wheel. Nothing is folded into a stack or a "more tools" button any more, and
  a tool you take with a key is scrolled into view. Each window size remembers where you left them.
- **Carry anything anywhere**: hold a tool or a command on either bar until it lifts, then carry it to another place,
  onto the other bar (pens can live on the top bar too, Zen or search on the rail), or away from both bars to take it
  off (the snackbar has Undo). Hold it without moving for its menu: move it, to the other bar, off the bars.
- **Group your tools**: carry a tool onto another and keep it there until a ring shows; let go, and both are one
  button that shows the one you used last (dots tell how many it holds). A tap takes it; tapped again, a group of two
  or three goes to the next tool, a bigger group opens its list. A group with commands in it (share, save, Zen, …)
  always opens its list, so nothing runs by accident. Hold a group for "Ungroup".
- **"+" adds things**: at the end of each bar, "+" offers a new tool (a pen, a highlighter, a shape, …) and everything
  that is on neither bar; a tap puts it at the end of that bar. On a phone it is a sheet. It replaces "Put back" and
  the phone's "My tools".
- **⋮ has everything**: whatever you put on your bars, every command stays in ⋮ (Document, Tools, View, Settings), so
  nothing can be lost.
- **Back to the first layout**: Settings → Pen → "Back to the first layout…" puts both bars back as they were at the
  first start; your tools stay.
- **Phones get the same bars**: the top bar sits under the title (beside it when the phone is held sideways), the rail
  is the dock at the bottom with "+" after your tools. Held sideways, the page number moved to the app bar, so the rail
  has room for your tools.
- **Full screen**: the floating rail's ⋯ lists what the top bar holds, and **New document**.
- **A text document's format bar** shows all its formatting and then the commands in one row that scrolls, undo and
  redo at its start.
- **The classic tool bar is gone**: the toolbox is the only way the tools are arranged. If you still used the classic
  bar, the toolbox is made from your tools of before at the first start: the first pen has your pen's color and width,
  the eraser erases as it did, the text box keeps your font. Gone with it: the tool square and the pen pill of full
  screen, the colors and widths in the bar, the bar's places and Settings → Pen → Tools' choice. The colors are chosen
  in a tool's editor (a tap on the tool in hand); the palette's source is shown in Settings → Pen → Colors.
- Your toolbox of 0.7.0 is kept as it was, with hand, select, snip and mark PDF text after your tools; write on the
  page, the setsquare, "the finger draws" and recording moved to the top bar. On a bar, holding a button lifts it: what
  its hold did before (present without controls, the templates of a new page, …) is the first entry of its menu.
- The toolbox's lines are sharp at 150 % scaling, and the floating toolbox no longer runs into the page pill.

## Zen

- **Zen** (the Zen button of the top bar, ⋮ → View → Zen, Ctrl+Alt+Z): only the page. The toolbox, the tool bar,
  the tabs, the sidebar's arrow and the pills go; a small faint dot stays in the lower left corner. The pen keeps
  writing, and P, H, E and T still take your tools. On Android the back gesture or button leaves Zen. A tap on the dot: **Show controls**, **Read only**, the page number
  (all pages), fit the width or the whole page. Esc leaves Zen. A very small window (a split screen, a pop-up window) is
  in Zen by itself; leave it there once and it stays that way for small windows.
- **Read only works anywhere now** (⋮ → View → Read only, or the dot's pill), not only in full screen. The lock in the
  corner is gone: when you try to write, a short note at the pen says the page is read only and how to write again.
  The left and right edges of the page still turn the pages.
- **Read** (Ctrl+Alt+R, ⋮ → View → Read) is Zen with read only, in full screen; Ctrl+Alt+R again or Esc ends it.
  **Present without controls** (Ctrl+F5) is presenting in Zen: its dot brings the controls back.
- Gone: the reading chrome and Settings → Display → "Controls at this size".
