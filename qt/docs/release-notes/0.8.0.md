Draft notes for the next pre-release of **xournal-qt** (not released yet; the version is set when it is cut).

## One way to arrange the tools

- **The classic tool bar is gone**: the toolbox is the only way the tools are arranged (your own pens, highlighters,
  erasers, shapes, text boxes and notes beside the page, each with its color and width). If you still used the classic
  bar, the toolbox is made from your tools of before at the first start: the first pen has your pen's color and width,
  the eraser erases as it did, the text box keeps your font. Gone with it: the tool square and the pen pill of full
  screen, the colors and widths in the bar, the bar's places (two rows, at a side, at the bottom) and Settings → Pen →
  Tools' choice. In full screen and while presenting the toolbox floats at the edge of the page (on a phone too); its
  ⋯ has present, read only, search, settings and the way back. The colors are chosen in a tool's editor (a tap on the
  tool in hand); the palette's source is shown in Settings → Pen → Colors.
- **The toolbox scrolls instead of folding**: your tools keep their order on every screen, from a wide desktop to the
  folded phone, and none of them is folded into a stack any more. When they do not all fit, the rail shows half of the
  next tool and a fade where there is more; swipe (or use the mouse wheel over a gap) to scroll. A tool you take with a
  key or from "My tools" is scrolled into view, and each window size remembers where you left the rail. On a phone the
  dock at the bottom is the same rail, scrolling sideways.
- **Group your tools**: hold a tool, carry it onto another and keep it there until a ring shows; let go, and both are
  one button that shows the one you used last (dots tell how many it holds). A tap takes it; tapped again, a group of
  two or three goes to the next tool, a bigger group opens its list. Hold a group for "Ungroup"; carry a tool out of
  its list to take it out. "Grouped" in the snackbar has Undo.
- **Hand, select, snip and mark PDF text are tools of the rail like yours**: carry them anywhere on it, group them
  (select with snip, for instance), or remove them ("+" puts them back). Write on the page, the setsquare, "the finger
  draws" and recording moved from the rail to the bar at the top (in full screen: the floating toolbox's ⋯), making
  room for your own tools. Your toolbox of 0.7.0 is kept as it was, with these four after your tools.
- A text document's format bar keeps its lists and inserts in sight on a tablet again (the commands go into »), the
  toolbox's lines are sharp at 150 % scaling, and the floating toolbox no longer runs into the page pill.
