## One way to arrange the tools

- **The classic tool bar is gone**: the toolbox is the only way the tools are arranged (your own pens, highlighters,
  erasers, shapes, text boxes and notes beside the page, each with its color and width). If you still used the classic
  bar, the toolbox is made from your tools of before at the first start: the first pen has your pen's color and width,
  the eraser erases as it did, the text box keeps your font. Gone with it: the tool square and the pen pill of full
  screen, the colors and widths in the bar, the bar's places (two rows, at a side, at the bottom) and Settings → Pen →
  Tools' choice. In full screen and while presenting the toolbox floats at the edge of the page (on a phone too); its
  ⋯ has present, read only, search, settings and the way back. The colors are chosen in a tool's editor (a tap on the
  tool in hand); the palette's source is shown in Settings → Pen → Colors.
- A text document's format bar keeps its lists and inserts in sight on a tablet again (the commands go into »), the
  toolbox's lines are sharp at 150 % scaling, and the floating toolbox no longer runs into the page pill.
