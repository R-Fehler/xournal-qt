Thirteenth alpha of **xournal-qt** (0.13.1: 0.13.0 was not published; its release run stopped at KDE neon's
archive, fixed here), a Qt 6 fork of Xournal++ for pen tablets and convertibles. It brings
a **Windows installer** that updates the installed version in place, opens `.xopp` and `.xoj` files with a
double-click and offers Xournal Qt for PDFs, and the **handwriting search on by default** on every platform. It is a
pre-release: see "Known limits".

## Windows: an installer

- **`xournal-qt-<version>-windows-setup-x64.exe`** installs Xournal Qt for you, without administrator rights, into
  `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\xournal-qt`: a Start menu entry, an entry in Apps → Installed apps with an uninstaller,
  and the program's icon in Explorer and the taskbar.
- **Updates**: the next version's installer updates the installed one in place, removing the old program files
  first. It asks you to close Xournal Qt if it is running. Your settings and libraries are never touched, and
  installing an older version over a newer one asks first.
- **File types**: `.xopp` and `.xoj` files open in Xournal Qt with a double-click (a choice in the installer, on by
  default), and Xournal Qt is in "Open with" for PDF files. **"Make Xournal Qt the default PDF app"** (off by
  default) opens Windows' settings on Xournal Qt's page, where you choose it for `.pdf` (Windows lets only you make
  that choice).
- It installs next to Xournal++ without touching it. The portable zip stays for those who prefer it.

## Handwriting search

- **Searching handwriting is on by default.** The model comes with the app, so nothing is downloaded: after the first
  start, Ctrl+F and the library's search find handwritten words. Switch it off in Settings → Search → "Search
  handwriting".
- **The library's handwriting is read on battery too**, on every platform (before: only on mains power on Linux and
  Windows, never on phones). It is still read one document at a time at the lowest priority, after the open documents,
  and waits while you write and while pages are drawn.

## Linux: the AppImage carries Qt 6.11

- **The AppImage is built on Ubuntu 22.04 with Qt 6.11 from qt.io** and goes through the whole test suite first (it
  was built with KDE neon's Qt 6.7). It runs on Ubuntu 22.04 and newer and on KDE neon, whatever Qt the system has.
- **No `.deb` for KDE neon / Ubuntu 22.04 any more**: KDE neon's archive for Ubuntu 22.04 broke the release of 0.13.0.
  Use the AppImage there. The packages for Debian 13 and Ubuntu 26.04 stay.

## Known limits

- **The installer was tried on GitHub's Windows runner only**, silently: install, opening a `.xopp` by its file type,
  an update, a refused downgrade, the refusal while the app runs, and the uninstaller. Its pages, the desktop
  shortcut and the "default PDF app" step on a real Windows machine are not checked yet (the device checklist,
  `qt/docs/testing/device-checklist.md`, has them).
- The installer and the program are **not signed**, so SmartScreen warns before the first start.
- Xournal Qt does not look for updates itself yet: download the next installer and run it.
- **Handwriting search on battery**: not yet measured on a laptop and a phone. A library document whose reading was
  stopped partway (the search switched off and on again) may be skipped until the next start.
- While presenting, a page narrower than the screen (an upright A4, a slide with its space for notes beside it) shows
  the neighbouring pages at its sides.
- **Android: the top bar scrolls into a blank area at its end** (from 0.8.0, not fixed yet).
- Plugins cannot be installed on Android yet; the function plotter comes with the app everywhere.
