Twelfth alpha of **xournal-qt**, a Qt 6 fork of Xournal++ for pen tablets and convertibles. This one reworks
**presenting with a second screen**: the laptop shows the slide as on one screen with your tools, you can zoom in to
write while the projector keeps the whole slide, and all pages are one key away. It is a pre-release: see "Known
limits".

## Presenting with a second screen

- **The presenter's console is presenting as on one screen**: the page fitted to the laptop's whole screen with its
  space for notes, and the toolbox at its edge to write on the slide. The panel at the right is gone.
- **A small pill at the top left** shows the minutes since the start ("12 min"). A tap opens it: the clock, the time
  with pause and back to 0:00, "Page 3 of 20", the next page, and the switches that were in the panel (notes for the
  audience, the audience follows my zoom, Fit, swap screens, End). A tap beside it or Esc closes it again.
- **All pages while presenting**: the grid button of the pill, or **G** (in either window; on one screen too, and in
  the floating toolbox's ⋯). Tap a page to present it; the projector keeps the current slide until then. Esc closes
  the grid first, the presentation goes on.
- **Zoom in on the console to write a detail**: the projector keeps the whole slide (and shows the ink at once).
  Zoomed in, swiping and scrolling stay on the slide; the next slide always comes whole. "The audience follows my zoom"
  is now **off by default** (switch it on in the pill or in Settings → Pen → Presenting).
- **The laser pointer is on the rail while presenting**: the first tools have one; a rail without one gets it at its
  head while presenting (hold it for "Keep it on the rail" or "Not while presenting"; also Settings → Pen →
  Presenting).
- Esc and Android's Back close the page grid before they leave full screen or end presenting, also outside presenting.

## Getting it

| Platform | File |
| --- | --- |
| Linux (KDE neon, Ubuntu 22.04) | `xournal-qt_0.12.0_amd64_neon-jammy.deb` |
| Linux (Debian 13, Ubuntu 25.04 and newer) | `xournal-qt_0.12.0_amd64_debian13.deb` |
| Any Linux | `xournal-qt-0.12.0-x86_64.AppImage` |
| Windows 10/11 (x64) | `xournal-qt-0.12.0-windows-x64.zip`: unpack anywhere and start `xournal-qt\bin\xournal-qt.exe` (no installer yet; SmartScreen may ask first: "More info" → "Run anyway") |
| macOS 15 or newer, Apple Silicon | `xournal-qt-0.12.0-macos-arm64.dmg`: not signed yet — on first start macOS refuses; then System Settings → Privacy & Security → "Open Anyway" (`qt/docs/development/macos.md`) |
| Android 9 or newer (64-bit ARM) | `xournal-qt-0.12.0-android-arm64.apk`: open it on the phone; it installs over 0.11.1 and keeps the library and the settings |

## Known limits

- **Tested with simulated screens only**: the new console with a real projector, a clicker and a touch tablet. See
  the device checklist (`qt/docs/testing/device-checklist.md`).
- While presenting, a page narrower than the screen (an upright A4, a slide with its space for notes beside it) shows
  the neighbouring pages at its sides.
- **Android: the top bar scrolls into a blank area at its end** (from 0.8.0, not fixed yet).
- Plugins cannot be installed on Android yet; the function plotter comes with the app everywhere.
