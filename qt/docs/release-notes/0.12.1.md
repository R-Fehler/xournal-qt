Twelfth alpha of **xournal-qt**, second build (0.12.1), a Qt 6 fork of Xournal++ for pen tablets and convertibles.
This one answers the first testers (xournalpp/xournalpp#7770): a **package for Ubuntu 26.04**, the **eraser end** of a
Wacom pen erasing as wide as the tool bar says, and a handful of fixes to the page sidebar, Save as, the library's
Import, the stabilizer and the **curtain and spotlight**. It is a pre-release: see "Known limits".

## Linux packages

- **A package for Ubuntu 26.04** (and Kubuntu 26.04): `xournal-qt_0.12.1_amd64_ubuntu26.04.deb`, built on Ubuntu
  26.04 with its Qt 6.10. Until now the releases said the Debian 13 package was also for Ubuntu 25.04 and newer, but
  Ubuntu 26.04 refuses it: it asks for `libjpeg62-turbo` and `libxml2` (Ubuntu 26.04 has `libjpeg-turbo8` and
  `libxml2-16`) and for exactly Debian's Qt 6.8.2 (`qt6-base-private-abi`, `qt6-declarative-private-abi`). A `.deb`
  fits only the system it was built on; the Debian 13 package is for Debian 13 only.

## What testers reported (xournalpp/xournalpp#7770)

- **Fixed: the pen's eraser end erased with an old width.** It kept the width the tool bar's eraser had when the app
  started, while its outline showed the new one (a Wacom CTL-672). Now it erases as wide as the tool bar's eraser,
  and so does a side button that erases.
- **Fixed: the page sidebar could not be opened with the top bar put away.** Its arrow at the left edge stays now.
- **Curtain and spotlight**: a button for each, which "+" (the catalog) offers under View, to put on the rail or the
  top bar; their handles look like a selection's (round, the knob that turns it red); and Settings → Pen →
  Presenting → **Darkness of the curtain and the spotlight** (100 % black, the default, down to 20 %: the page
  shows through dimmed).
- **Save as… on the Save button**: hold it (or right-click it). ⋮ and Ctrl+Shift+S have it as before.
- **Import** in the library says "Import" beside its icon where the header has room.
- **The stabilizer**: with the arithmetic mean the buffer size slider goes from 2 to 20, the sizes that help, over
  its whole length (the Gaussian weights keep 2 to 100).

## Known limits

- **Tested with simulated input only**: the eraser end on a real Wacom pen, the curtain's new handles with a finger,
  and its darkness on a projector. See the device checklist (`qt/docs/testing/device-checklist.md`).
- The Ubuntu 26.04 package was built and installed in a container; a try on a real Ubuntu 26.04 is welcome.
- While presenting, a page narrower than the screen (an upright A4, a slide with its space for notes beside it) shows
  the neighbouring pages at its sides.
- **Android: the top bar scrolls into a blank area at its end** (from 0.8.0, not fixed yet).
- Plugins cannot be installed on Android yet; the function plotter comes with the app everywhere.
